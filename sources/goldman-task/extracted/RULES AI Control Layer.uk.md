# Terms and conditions of the "AI CONTROL LAYER" competition

1. These terms and conditions (hereinafter referred to as the Terms) define the rules and conditions of the "AI Control Layer" competition within the HackYeah hackathon and are an annex to the general terms and conditions of the HackYeah hackathon.

2. The hackathon Sponsor and the party promising the prize within the meaning of art. 921 § 3 of the Civil Code (Journal of Laws 2017, item 459) is Proidea Sp. z o.o., with its registered office at Kraków 30-418, ul. Zakopiańska 9; registration numbers: NIP (Taxpayers ID no) — 6793088842, REGON (Business ID no) — 122769022; KRS (National Court Register) number — 0000448243. Contact address: biuro@proidea.org.pl.

3. Details of the task will be presented at the start of the competition.

4. Prizes in competitions organized by the Organizer will be issued within 90 days after the announcement of the results, unless the rules of the respective competition provide otherwise.

5. Participation in the "AI Control Layer" competition may be individual or in teams of up to six people who started solving the competition task no earlier than 23:00 on October 3 and forwarded it for evaluation no later than 23:00 on October 4. The solution submitted for evaluation must include:

   a) the project title;

   b) the team name;

   c) the list of team members (1–6 people);

   d) the project description;

   e) a PDF presentation of no more than 10 slides; screenshots, a code repository, demo links, graphic materials, and other materials related to the project or suggested in the detailed task description may also be included. Task solutions should be submitted on the HackTribe platform in English or Polish.

> Translator's note: the original states "11:00 PM" for both dates; here it is rendered as 23:00 without correcting the source.

<!-- Page 2 -->

6. Participants who are related to or affiliated with members of the "AI Control Layer" competition jury, as well as employees of the party promising the prize, are excluded from participation.

7. The total prize pool of the competition is PLN 15,000 (including tax) and is distributed as follows:

   - 1st place: PLN 6,000 (including tax);
   - 2nd place: PLN 5,000 (including tax);
   - 3rd place: PLN 4,000 (including tax).

8. The goal of the competition is to present solutions to the problem, formulated as an open task. The solutions of the respective tasks will be evaluated in two phases:

   a) phase 1 — evaluation of the projects submitted on the HackTribe platform by a commission appointed for the specific task, consisting of at least three mentors;

   b) phase 2 — evaluation by the jury of the finalists' live presentations. The presentations (pitches) will be given by the teams selected in phase 1. It is emphasized that the composition of the phase 1 commission and the phase 2 jury may coincide, but they do not have to be the same individuals.

9. The jury members elect a chairman from among themselves.

10. In the event of an equal number of votes in both phases, the jury's vote is decisive.

> Translator's note: the original says "the Jury's vote", not "the chairman's vote"; the ambiguity is preserved.

11. Judging criteria:

   - Robustness of the solution and quality of guardrails — 30%.
   - Architecture and performance efficiency — 20%.
   - Security reporting — 20%.
   - Completeness of the self-testing suite — 20%.
   - Practical implementability and scalability — 10%.

12. To receive the award, the project must score at least 50% of the points in phase 1. The decisions of the jury and its members are final and cannot be appealed.

<!-- Page 3 -->

13. Any alterations or revisions made after the expiry of the statutory time are illegal. The jury will not consider any alterations or modifications made after the expiry of the statutory time.

14. The proprietary copyrights to the awarded solution are not transferred to the competition Sponsor.

15. The jury members will be published on the HackYeah communication platform (Discord) no later than October 4.
